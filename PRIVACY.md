# Privacy Policy for Countdown

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Countdown is a Chrome extension that shows live countdowns to your deadlines and events in Chrome's side panel.

## What data Countdown handles

- **Your items:** the titles, dates, start and end times, locations, notes, repeat rules and categories you enter, and when you completed items.
- **Your settings:** the countdown unit, light/dark mode and colour theme you chose, whether the toolbar icon shows the time left, which sections are collapsed, and the calendar view and categories you chose to show.
- **Calendars you import:** events from .ics files you choose or calendar links you paste.

## How it's stored

Everything above is stored on your device, in Chrome's extension storage. The toolbar icon's countdown and badge are worked out on your device from the same data.

## Sync between your computers

Sync is on by default, and you can turn it off on each computer (Appearance → Sync with your other computers). While it's on, your items, repeating series, categories and display settings are also stored in Chrome sync (`chrome.storage.sync`): Chrome keeps that copy in your Google account and gives it to your other computers signed in to the same Chrome profile with Chrome sync on. It is Chrome's own sync, covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); it never passes through a server of ours, and the developer never receives it. If Chrome sync is off, or you aren't signed in to Chrome, nothing leaves your computer.

Which sections are collapsed, the calendar view, and the completion history kept for statistics stay on each computer. The first time a computer syncs, Countdown keeps a copy of that computer's list on it for 30 days, so you can restore it.

## Calendar links

When you paste a calendar link and press Fetch, Chrome asks whether Countdown may read that website. If you allow it, Countdown downloads that one calendar file directly from the server you chose, without sending cookies, and shows you a preview. Nothing else on that site is read, and nothing about you is sent to it beyond the request itself. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Chrome's extension settings (chrome://extensions → Countdown → Details → Site access).

## Exports

Exporting saves an .ics file to your computer, or opens a prefilled event in Google Calendar in a new tab. That event's details go to Google only when you open the link, and are covered by Google's privacy policy.

## What Countdown doesn't do

Countdown has no account, server, analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not read the pages you visit, collect your browsing history, or sell or share any data.

## Deleting your data

Remove items in the panel (with sync on, they're removed on your other computers too), or uninstall the extension to delete everything it stored on that computer. To delete what's in Chrome sync, remove your items while sync is on, or reset Chrome sync for your Google account at [chrome.google.com/sync](https://chrome.google.com/sync).

## Changes

If these practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above changed before the new version is published.
